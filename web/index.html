KidneyLoop

Kidney paired-donation cycles and chains, chosen on a Relate graph. Every number on this page comes from Cypher running in this tab on the committed store.

All patients and donors are synthetic. This is a demonstration, not a clinical tool.

How it works

  1. Rules are graph data. Blood types and antibody-to-antigen links are nodes and relationships, so Cypher derives who can give to whom.
  2. Cypher finds the cycles. Every 2-way and 3-way exchange is a path that closes on itself.
  3. An exact solver picks the plan. It builds chains from the compatible arcs and writes the chosen exchanges back as a MatchRun.
  4. Every answer is a path. Ask why a patient was matched or missed, and see the cycle and who blocks it.
