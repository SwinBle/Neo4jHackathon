Kidney exchanges, found on a graph

When a patient's donor cannot give to them, pairs can swap. Each donor gives to the next pair's patient and the loop closes. KidneyLoop finds these loops and chains with Cypher on a Relate graph and shows the path behind every match and every miss.

Synthetic data. All patients and donors are generated. This is a demonstration, not a clinical tool.

    How it works

    1. Rules are graph data. Blood types and antibody-to-antigen links are nodes and relationships, so Cypher derives who can give to whom.
    2. Cypher finds the loops. Every 2-way and 3-way exchange is a path that closes on itself.
    3. An exact solver picks the plan. It builds chains from altruistic donors and writes the chosen exchanges back to the graph.
    4. Every answer is a path. Ask why a patient was matched or missed, and see the loop and who blocks it.
